What an EV charger installation covers

The work depends on the home and the charger. An electrician can review the electrical panel, the proposed charger location, and the route for wiring, then explain what the installation calls for. The plan may be straightforward, or it may involve additional electrical work such as a panel upgrade.

Key facts to discuss:
- Scope: The charger, its location, wiring route, and any panel work needed.
- Timing: The schedule depends on the home, the installation scope, and appointment availability. Ask for a project-specific estimate of timing.
- Cost factors: Charger and electrical equipment, wiring distance, panel capacity, and the amount of labor all affect the overall cost. Ask for costs based on your setup rather than relying on a general estimate.
- Provider comparison: Ask who will assess the panel, what work is included, whether additional work could be needed, and how the electrician will explain the plan before starting.

You may hear a few technical terms. The electrical panel distributes power to circuits in the home. Panel capacity describes whether the existing system can support the planned electrical load. A circuit is the path that carries power to equipment. Ask the electrician to explain any other terms in the estimate.
